The Legend of the Jersey Devil

The Pine Barrens

The Jersey Devil is one of the most famous legends in New Jersey folklore. The creature is said to roam the Pine Barrens, a large forested region filled with mystery and strange stories dating back hundreds of years.

According to legend, the Jersey Devil was born as the cursed thirteenth child of Mother Leeds and transformed into a terrifying winged creature.
