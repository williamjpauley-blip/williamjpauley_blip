Famous Jersey Devil Sightings

Encounters in the Pine Barrens

Over the past two centuries, hundreds of people have claimed to witness the Jersey Devil roaming the Pine Barrens of southern New Jersey. Reports describe eerie screams, massive wings, glowing eyes, and strange tracks appearing in the snow.

During the famous 1909 sightings, schools reportedly closed, businesses shut down, and frightened residents armed themselves after widespread reports of a flying creature.

Reported Sightings

Year Location Event Reported By
1909 Leeds Point Flying creature over rooftops Town residents
1927 Salem County Mysterious hoof prints discovered Farm workers
1951 Gibbstown Strange screams heard at night Local families
1997 Pine Barrens Dark winged figure sighted Campers

Witness Description

"It moved quickly through the trees with a scream unlike anything I had ever heard before."

Common Characteristics

  • Horse-like head
  • Large bat wings
  • Glowing red eyes
  • Hooved feet
  • Terrifying screams
