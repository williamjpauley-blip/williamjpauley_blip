The Legend Continues

The Jersey Devil remains one of the most famous legends in American folklore. Stories of the creature have been passed down for generations throughout New Jersey. Many people claim to have heard strange screams deep within the Pine Barrens forest.

Witnesses often describe the creature as having bat-like wings, glowing red eyes, hooves, and a terrifying scream.
